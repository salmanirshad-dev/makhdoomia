About the school

A high school at the heart of Warpora.

01 — Introduction

Strengthening the academic foundation of the Dangarpora belt.

Makhdoomia Model High School sits at Warpora, Dangarpora, in Sopore — the “Apple Town of Kashmir” in Baramulla district. With a history rooted in community service and education, the school serves families across the surrounding area, guided by Chairman Sajad Ahmad Nadvi and Principal Shabir Ahmad War.

03 — Gallery
From the ground

Students, staff and the wider community.

In May 2026, the 179th Battalion of the CRPF organised a Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyan (Drug-Free India Campaign) at Makhdoomia Model High School, Warpora, under its Civic Action Programme. Students presented a skit portraying the devastating impact of substance abuse on individuals, families and society.

Commanding Officer Vinay Kumar Choudhary and Principal of HSS Botingoo, Athar Hussain, attended as chief guests, alongside school Principal Shabir Ahmad War and General Secretary Abdul Rasheed — reflecting the school's active role in community welfare.
